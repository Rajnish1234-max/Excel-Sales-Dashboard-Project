# 📊 ABC Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview
The **ABC Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel. The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, revenue, profitability, and regional business trends through data visualization and Pivot Tables.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful insights, helping stakeholders monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profitability performance.
- Track monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Identify top-performing countries based on revenue.
- Understand revenue distribution across different sales channels.
- Compare business performance across geographical regions.
- Build an interactive dashboard for dynamic data analysis.

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Revenue
- Total Gross Profit
- Gross Profit Margin
- Total Units Sold
- Total Orders
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

### 2. Monthly Revenue & Profit Analysis
Visualizes monthly revenue and gross profit trends to identify business performance patterns throughout the year.

### 3. Top 5 Countries by Revenue
Highlights the top five revenue-generating countries to understand geographical sales performance.

### 4. Revenue Share by Channel
Analyzes revenue contribution from different sales channels:
- Corporate
- Marketplace
- Online Store
- Retail Partners

### 5. Region-wise Comparison
Compares revenue distribution across different European regions.

### 6. Country-wise Map Chart
Provides geographical visualization of revenue performance across countries.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

## 🎛️ Interactive Features
The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to filter and explore business performance based on:
- Product Category
- Country
- Quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)

These filters enable dynamic analysis and make the dashboard more user-friendly.
